## Recommendation Gate

Before presenting any role as a target, answer:

1. Is it absent from the applied/in-process table?
2. Is it absent from local artifact exclusions, or did live Teal prove it is not applied?
3. Does the listing avoid hard experience requirements?
4. Does pay plausibly beat or improve on the Farm House benchmark?
5. Is the next action clear and non-duplicative?

If any answer is no, do not recommend it.
