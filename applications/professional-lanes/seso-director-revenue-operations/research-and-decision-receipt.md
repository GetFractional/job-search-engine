## Company Snapshot

Seso is modernizing the agricultural workforce back office. Its current site describes an all-in-one system for farms to hire, manage, and retain workers. The company states that it serves customers in all 50 states, including 35 of the 100 largest agricultural employers in the United States. Its product covers digital onboarding, HR administration, H-2A filing and compliance, payroll, paycards, and worker services.

The company is expanding from a strong H-2A and compliance wedge into a broader HR, payroll, insurance, and financial-services platform. The 2024 Series B announcement described an AI-enabled payroll product, banking and remittance services, and a comprehensive agribusiness HR platform. The current job description says the company has raised more than $60 million.

Company and investor statistics are useful for internal context. They should not be presented as Matt's accomplishments.

## Market Context

### Researched facts

- Seso says farms have modernized production technology faster than back-office workforce systems.
- The product addresses labor access, seasonal-workforce administration, H-2A complexity, payroll, insurance, onboarding, and compliance.
- Seso's 2024 funding announcement framed labor shortage and H-2A administrative complexity as urgent operating problems for farms.
- The company combines software and human-enabled services. Its software and service economics should not be treated as one undifferentiated SaaS motion.

### Inferences

- Moving from a visa-automation wedge into a broader platform increases GTM complexity across products, buyer types, renewals, expansion, implementation, and customer success.
- A regulated, seasonal market makes clean data, capacity planning, handoffs, forecasting, and adoption especially important.
- The company likely needs RevOps to evolve from tool support into a governed operating system shared by Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance, and leadership.
- The AI mandate likely reflects both cost pressure and a desire to improve GTM productivity without scaling headcount linearly.

### Competitive pressure

- legacy spreadsheets and on-premise farm back-office systems,

- vertical agriculture workforce software,

The reviewed sources do not disclose Seso's complete named competitor set. Do not invent a competitor list in external assets or interviews.

## Why The Role Likely Exists

The company appears to need one accountable leader to turn a growing collection of GTM tools, data, workflows, and AI opportunities into one operating system.

The role is expected to:

- govern Salesforce and HubSpot across Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success,
- improve data quality, system integration, and adoption,
- build dashboards, forecasts, and pipeline reporting for GTM leadership and Finance,
- own funnel definitions from top of funnel through expansion,
- improve routing, opportunity management, handoffs, renewals, and compensation processes,
- manage a small specialist team,
- identify, evaluate, deploy, measure, and scale high-value AI use cases.

This is not a marketing-campaign role. It is a GTM systems, analytics, adoption, and governance role with a significant Finance interface.
