## 1. Role And Company Snapshot

Seso provides software and services that help agribusinesses recruit, onboard, pay, insure, and support their workforce. Its H-2A automation and compliance wedge has expanded into a broader HR, payroll, insurance, and financial-services platform.

The role owns the GTM systems and reporting layer across Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance, and leadership. It also manages a small team including a Salesforce administrator and marketing operations contractor.

The most distinctive part of the mandate is not simply using AI. It is building a disciplined process to identify, evaluate, deploy, measure, and scale AI tools across the revenue motion.

### Objection: How deep are you in Salesforce?

**Response:**

My deepest hands-on CRM implementation leadership has been in Zoho One and Microsoft Dynamics 365. ## 9. AI Operating Principles

If asked about an AI-first GTM organization, use this structure:

1. Start with a business constraint, not a tool.
2. Capture the current baseline for time, cost, quality, and outcome.
3. Select use cases with repeatable inputs and meaningful volume.
4. Define privacy, compliance, human review, and failure handling.
5. Pilot with one owner and a fixed evaluation window.
6. Measure adoption and quality, not just tool activation.
7. Scale only when the result survives real workflow conditions.
8. Retire tools and automations that add complexity without measurable value.

HireHawk is the proof example. Do not claim direct experience with every AI use case listed in Seso's posting.

## 10. First-90-Day Hypothesis

### Days 1 to 30: learn and baseline

- meet Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance, systems owners, and customer-facing teams,

- review definitions, dashboard trust, forecast process, handoffs, renewal workflows, and compensation cadence,

### Days 31 to 60: prioritize and prove

- publish a sequenced RevOps roadmap with impact, effort, risk, and dependencies,
- fix one high-value reporting or data-quality issue,
- clarify ownership and acceptance criteria for one broken handoff,
- run one or two bounded AI evaluations with human review and measurement,
- establish a practical operating cadence for the team and stakeholders.

### Days 61 to 90: stabilize and scale

- deliver a trusted minimum executive view,
- document governance for critical workflows and definitions,
- scale only the AI pilots that show measurable value,
- create a stack rationalization and adoption plan,
- agree with GTM and Finance on the next two quarters of priorities.

This is a discussion hypothesis. Matt should ask what is already working before presenting it as the answer.
