# My Way Ahead Offer and Journey

1. Read the company OS and newest relevant dated addendum.

Use Alen Sultanic and Alex Hormozi as internal lenses for recognition, value, mechanism, belief shift, friction reduction, offer structure, and risk reversal. Do not name-drop them in public copy. Do not copy proprietary language or use manipulation, guarantees, false scarcity, fabricated proof, or fear exploitation.

## Define the customer movement

Describe:

- trigger and awareness state;
- desired practical and emotional outcome;
- current failed alternatives and repeated work;
- belief that blocks action;
- smallest credible first value;
- product mechanism and proof;
- objection, risk, unknown, and recovery;
- one primary action and no more than two supporting actions.

Optimize for the movement `overwhelmed, overlooked, uncertain -> understood, selective, prepared, in control` while staying claim-safe.

## Design the journey

- Lead with finding better-fit jobs and building a strong honest pursuit; keep pasted-job checking secondary.
- Understand the person once, let them correct the truth, define a Job Standard, discover credible career paths, choose active monitoring paths, see verified jobs, decide, prepare, approve, and learn.
- Minimize clicks. Every click must create a necessary decision, consent, or new information state.
- Use stable logged-in navigation. Settings never replaces primary navigation.
- Preserve safe setup state across refreshes and device interruptions. Never render a blank or unrelated destination; recover at the first missing truthful choice while retaining prior selections.
- Discover all credible career paths; use entitlements to govern active monitoring and costly analysis, not truth visibility.
- Give repeated path, decision, and preference controls context-specific accessible names even when their visible labels stay compact.
- Resolve resumes deterministically: job-specific assignment, then career-path assignment, then master.
- Keep profile imports, deductions, corrections, confidence, duplicates, and provenance reviewable.
- For visual changes, use the Product Design audit and ideation workflow before implementation.

## Build the offer architecture

For each offer state the customer job, included outcome, entitlement, cadence, price hypothesis, renewal behavior, fulfillment owner, variable-cost ceiling, support ceiling, gross-contribution target, failure or downgrade behavior, and stop condition.

Rules:

- Separate software, human service, and affiliate economics.
- Do not sell a human service Matt cannot currently fulfill.
- A referral must remain commission-neutral, disclosed, quality-reviewed, privacy-reviewed, and Board-approved before promotion.
- Gate recurring or multi-path analysis, not basic file storage or truthful career-path discovery.
- Use contextual cross-sells only after the product has identified the related need.
- Down-sells must reduce scope or cadence without silently weakening analysis integrity.
- Treat every price as private test copy until Matt approves the exact public test.
- Recognize prepaid cash across the service period when reporting MRR.

## Economics contract

Calculate payment fees, model and data cost, storage, email, support, correction and rework, free-user subsidy, refunds, affiliate revenue, and contribution separately. Show assumptions. Define cohort-level stop conditions before launch. Do not use blended revenue to hide labor or inference cost.

## Acquisition contract

- Prefer a few product-led pages that produce a useful result over many thin pages.
- Answer the query immediately; expose methodology, limitations, sources, and last-updated date.
- Keep important acquisition content indexable outside login.
- Use structured data only when visible content and source rights qualify.
- Validate search demand before scaling pages. Do not invent volume.
- Do not scrape LinkedIn or aggregator pages, and do not publish job markup without lawful, current job-detail pages.
