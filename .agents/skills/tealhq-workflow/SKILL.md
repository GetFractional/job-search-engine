# TealHQ Competitor Research

## Governing boundary

Way Ahead is Matt's job-search operating system. This skill may inspect Teal
only as a competitor and convert observed patterns into provider-neutral product
requirements.

Never use Teal to:

- find, save, score, stage, or track Matt's current jobs;
- create, edit, export, or store Matt's current application assets;
- hold contacts, follow-ups, interview state, or outcomes;
- populate an employer form, upload a file, send outreach, or submit;
- define Way Ahead's product architecture, schema, routes, customer copy, or
  approval model.

Do not mutate Teal for competitor research. Historical Teal operating documents
are evidence of prior workflows, not current instructions.

## Required sources

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `job-search-scenarios`
3. The exact Teal surface Matt asks to study
4. Current Way Ahead journey and strategy documents

## Process

1. Define the customer job or product question the comparison must answer.
2. Inspect only the smallest relevant Teal surface.
3. Record the observed flow, labels, clicks, states, strengths, friction, and
   accessibility risks without entering or changing Matt's data.
4. Separate direct observation from inference and stale historical notes.
5. Translate useful patterns into provider-neutral Way Ahead requirements.
6. Reject any pattern that conflicts with Way Ahead's truth, privacy,
   multi-user isolation, provenance, or exact-action approval controls.
7. Preserve differentiation. Do not clone Teal or let its current feature set
   become Way Ahead's roadmap by default.

## Output

- Product question
- Exact Teal surface and checked date
- Observed strengths
- Observed friction and accessibility risks
- Way Ahead requirement or decision affected
- What not to copy
- Evidence limits

## Safety

Use read-only competitor observation. Do not bypass login, CAPTCHA, permissions,
or paywalls. Do not expose secrets or personal data. Do not mutate Teal, contact
anyone, populate a form, upload, or submit.
