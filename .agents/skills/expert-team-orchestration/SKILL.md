# Expert Team Orchestration Skill

## Project Defaults
- Start with `job-search-scenarios` when the user asks to find, score, prepare, approve, or later submit a job pursuit through Way Ahead.
- Use Google Chrome for signed-in employer, LinkedIn, or job-board work when login state, Cloudflare, challenge prompts, or extension behavior matter. Observe Teal only when Matt explicitly requests bounded, read-only competitor research; never mutate it or use it for Matt's operating state.
- Keep Way Ahead as the operating system for jobs, analyses, pursuits, assets, approvals, outcomes, and follow-ups. Teal is competitor research only and may not hold or mutate Matt's current job-search state.
- For product strategy, use native, provider-neutral objects. Teal may supply bounded competitor evidence but may not define product architecture, routes, domain objects, MVP dependencies, or customer-facing copy.
- Preserve claim safety with the Canonical Profile and Metrics Ledger before external-facing metrics, bullets, cover letters, application answers, or outreach.
- Stop before application submission, outreach, references, or external compensation negotiation unless the user explicitly approves. Voluntary self-ID, race, gender, veteran, disability, and clearance fields may be answered from standing defaults when no contradictory instruction exists.

## Purpose
For each major job-search task, think like a coordinated expert team while producing one clear output.

## Expert Lenses
- Executive recruiter
- ATS resume architect
- Compensation strategist
- GTM / RevOps strategist
- Ecommerce growth strategist
- Lifecycle / CRM strategist
- Company research analyst
- Market intelligence analyst
- Hiring manager psychologist
- Executive communications strategist
- Interview coach
- AI workflow architect

## Inputs
- User task
- Role intake
- JD
- Research brief
- Fit scorecard
- Asset draft, if applicable

## Process
1. Identify which expert lenses matter for the task.
2. Use recruiter and ATS lenses to keep fit obvious and parseable.
3. Use strategy lenses to diagnose the business problem.
4. Use psychology and communications lenses to reduce friction and improve clarity.
5. Use compensation lens when comp, offer, or leverage is involved.
6. Use QA lens before anything external.

## Output
- Coordinated recommendation
- Key risks
- Best next action
- Draft asset or decision, if requested

## Safety
This is an internal reasoning framework. Do not create verbose "expert team" theatrics in external assets.
