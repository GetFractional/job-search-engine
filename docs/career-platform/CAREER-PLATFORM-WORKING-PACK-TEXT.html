The product decision

Build an independent career decision system, not another job board, application-volume tool, or generic AI matcher. The recurring value is maintaining a user-approved Career Baseline, verifying opportunity integrity, mapping requirements to source-backed proof, comparing career economics and pursuit effort, and preparing external action only after approval.

Selected customer journey

  1. Radar Setup defines what should be monitored and when the user should be interrupted.
  2. Each dated Radar Brief explains the few opportunities that deserve attention or confirms that none do.
  3. Opportunity Review separates proven, plausible, missing, risky, and excluded evidence.
  4. The Decision Receipt records the read-only recommendation, main risk, user decision, and one next action.
  5. A native Pursuit owns preparation; external-action approval remains separate and exact.

Defensible wedge

The platform wins by making the entire decision contract inspectable: baseline, job integrity, proof lineage, career economics, uncertainty, effort, user decision, external-action approval, and calibration. Scanning and alerts alone are not enough to justify a subscription.

A dark theme is not a simple inversion of the light theme.

Monetization sequence

  1. Demonstrate one Career Baseline diagnostic or Decision Receipt.
  2. Validate judgment through a concierge-assisted paid beta before engineering continuous monitoring.
  3. Charge recurring fees for maintained, verified decisions, not alerts alone.
  4. Offer a higher-value Pursuit Sprint when an opportunity clears the evidence and economics bar.
  5. Use observed decisions, corrections, applications, interviews, offers, and no-action weeks to calibrate the product without inventing causal claims.

What the paid beta must prove

  • Users change or defend a real career decision because of the Decision Receipt.
  • Users value verified restraint during a no-opportunity week.
  • The product reduces wasted pursuits without suppressing credible upside.
  • Users will pay for independent judgment despite free and low-cost scanning alternatives.
  • The recurring service remains useful after a user gets a new job.

Market position

The moat is the inspectable decision contract

Always-on scanning, job radar, ranked matches, career intelligence, reason explanations, resume generation, and one next action are active competitor claims. The product must make this combination observable:

  1. user-approved Career Baseline,
  2. canonical employer, job, freshness, and duplicate integrity,
  3. versioned proof lineage for every externally usable claim,
  4. career economics compared with the user's current position,
  5. visible uncertainty and pursuit effort,
  6. a portable Decision Receipt,
  7. separate user decision and external-action approval,
  8. calibration from corrections and observed outcomes.
