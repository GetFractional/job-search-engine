# Target Personas And Customer Journey

Date: 2026-07-17

## Launch Focus

Do not launch to everyone looking for work. Start with experienced U.S. professionals who:

- have roughly 7 to 20 years of experience,
- earn or target about $90,000 to $250,000,
- have enough evidence to support a real decision,
- could credibly pursue more than one adjacent lane or a meaningful level-up,
- value time, compensation, benefits, and career optionality,
- dislike mass applying and unsupported claims.

The fastest route to revenue and evidence is the **Active Optimizer**. The strongest recurring-subscription persona is the **Ambitious Pathfinder**.

## Four Core Personas

| Persona | Current state | What they are trying to decide | What they already tried | Why it failed | Best first offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Active Optimizer** | Actively searching; target lane is reasonably clear | Which live role deserves focused effort, and how do I make the strongest truthful case? | Teal, LinkedIn, ChatGPT, resume builders, ATS scanners, spreadsheets | Tools optimize pieces but do not join job integrity, strategic fit, proof, economics, and action | Paid Pursuit Sprint or guided beta |
| **Ambitious Pathfinder** | Employed or quietly open; more than one plausible lane | Is anything materially better than where I am now? | Alerts, recruiters, saved searches, newsletters, occasional browsing | Too noisy, too generic, too much effort, and no comparison with the current career baseline | Opportunity Radar subscription after one free Decision Receipt |
| **Focused Climber** | Employed or monitoring; target level or role is clear | Am I actually ready for the level-up, and will the market believe my case? | Salary sites, networking, resume scanners, coaching | Readiness advice is detached from live roles and source-backed proof | Targeted Radar plus occasional Pursuit Sprint |
| **Career Reframer** | Active or urgent; several adjacent paths are possible | Which version of my experience is credible, valuable, and worth the switching cost? | Assessments, broad searches, generic coaching, multiple resumes | Possibility is mistaken for market credibility; options are not compared consistently | Guided Role Discovery, then Sprint and Radar |

## Behavioral Overlays

These are not separate acquisition segments. They change the product experience:

- **Burnt-Out:** needs low setup, visible progress, and no duplicate work.
- **Sophisticated Skeptic:** needs source lineage, corrections, uncertainty, and reversibility.
- **Privacy-Sensitive Professional:** needs quiet monitoring, no public signal, data control, and explicit external approval.
- **Signal Builder:** lacks enough proof for the initial decision product and may need a later career-capital product.

## Primary Jobs To Be Done

### Passive or employed

> When I am focused on my current life and work, help me know whether a real opportunity is materially better, so I do not miss upside or waste time running a constant search.

### Active search

> When I need a better role, help me choose and pursue the strongest credible opportunities, so my effort goes toward jobs I can win and should want.

### Level-up

> When I am considering a more senior or better-paid role, show me whether my evidence supports it and what would invalidate the move, so I can act with confidence rather than hope.

## Customer Journey

| Stage | User question | Product job | Trust proof | Conversion or success signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Trigger | Am I underpaid, stuck, at risk, or missing something better? | Name the decision problem without manufacturing fear | Specific examples of wasted pursuit or missed opportunity | User recognizes the problem as current and costly |
| 2. Diagnostic | Is this better than my current patchwork? | Produce one useful Career Baseline or Decision Receipt from a real role | Sources, uncertainty, corrections, and no invented claims | User says the result changed or clarified a real decision |
| 3. Baseline and proof | What counts as better, and what can I prove? | Build user-approved role lanes, constraints, compensation floor, career capital, and proof | Fact versions, provenance, exclusions, and user approval | Baseline is complete enough to govern recommendations |
| 4. Radar | Is anything worth attention now? | Monitor narrow sources, suppress noise, and surface only material opportunities | Reviewed, surfaced, suppressed, freshness, and source status | User opens, trusts, or values a no-action result |
| 5. Review | Why should I pursue or pass? | Compare integrity, requirements, evidence, economics, risk, uncertainty, and effort | Inspectable requirement-to-proof lineage and main invalidating risk | User makes a confident pursue, watch, or pass decision |
| 6. Pursuit | What is the strongest truthful way to compete? | Prepare a grounded resume strategy, answers, outreach option, and interview plan | Every external claim maps to approved evidence | Package becomes approval-ready without unsupported claims |
| 7. Approval and handoff | What will happen outside the product? | Separate user decision from submit, send, share, or tracker approval | Exact destination, asset, action, and approval receipt | User knowingly authorizes the exact external action |
| 8. Outcome learning | What did the market teach us? | Capture response, screen, interview, offer, rejection, and user correction | Observed outcome separated from inference | Thresholds improve without false causal claims |
| 9. New baseline | What would be better now? | Reset expectations after a job change and keep the Radar useful | New compensation, role, constraints, and career capital are approved | Subscription remains useful after placement |

## Offer Ladder

1. **Free diagnostic:** one real Career Baseline comparison or Decision Receipt.
2. **Opportunity Radar:** recurring low-noise monitoring and decision support. Current hypothesis: $19 to $29 monthly, not validated.
3. **Pursuit Sprint:** one approval-ready high-value pursuit. Current hypothesis: about $99 self-guided or higher with operator help.
4. **Guided beta:** concierge-assisted decision and pursuit for users who need speed or have complex proof. Current hypothesis: $399 to $599, subject to interviews and paid demand.

Scanning, alerts, match scores, resumes, and cover letters alone do not justify these prices. The paid contrast is independent judgment, proof, integrity, economics, uncertainty, and continuity.

## Acquisition Messages To Test

### Active Optimizer

> Stop treating every plausible job like a good opportunity. See which role is real, worth it, and defensible before you spend another night applying.

### Ambitious Pathfinder

> Stay focused on the job you have. We will tell you when something genuinely improves your options, and show you why.

### Focused Climber

> Know whether your evidence supports the next level before a hiring team decides for you.

### Career Reframer

> Compare the paths your experience can credibly unlock, not just the titles a search engine can find.

## Most Important Objections

- `Why would I pay when alerts and AI tools are free?`
- `Will this just give me another match score I cannot trust?`
- `How do I know it will not invent or exaggerate my experience?`
- `Will my employer or recruiters know I am looking?`
- `How much setup and maintenance will this require?`
- `Does it actually find better opportunities, or only rewrite applications?`
- `What happens after I get a job?`

The beta must answer these with observable product behavior, not reassurance copy.

## Launch Non-Targets

- buyers who primarily want mass apply or autonomous submission,
- resume-template-only or keyword-score-only buyers,
- users unwilling to verify evidence or approve external actions,
- hourly, gig, or shift-market discovery in the first release,
- employer and recruiter workflows in the first release,
- legal, immigration, or credential adjudication,
- users with too little evidence for a useful decision.
