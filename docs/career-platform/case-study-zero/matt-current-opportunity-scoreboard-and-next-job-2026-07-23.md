## Product Findings From the Real Application Journey

This case study creates five non-negotiable product requirements:

1. **Separate fit from action readiness.** A higher-fit role can be the wrong next action when required proof is unresolved.
2. **Inspect the live application before generating assets.** Wpromote's forecasting yes/no gate materially changes whether the pursuit is worth the effort.
3. **Bind analysis and assets to the exact posting version.** A new or changed source must invalidate an older recommendation and asset package.
4. **Turn missing facts into small, answerable gates.** Ask Matt one precise question with `Yes`, `No`, and `I need help answering`, then preserve the answer and provenance.
5. **Do not force unnecessary assets.** No cover-letter slot means no cover letter unless Matt requests a strategic exception.

The Today view should show at least:

- fit score,
- freshness and last verification,
- career path,
- hard-gate status,
- asset readiness,
