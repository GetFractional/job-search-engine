# 06 Delivery Roadmap and Founder Dogfooding

## Delivery decision

Run two coordinated tracks without allowing one to block the other:

1. **Career outcome track:** help Matt pursue the best credible live opportunities now.
2. **Company build-and-validation track:** build the owned product in thin vertical slices, instrument the method, and learn from a narrow paid beta.

Matt's job search must not wait for the full product. Product development must not use Matt's result as proof that the company works for everyone. Real-user feedback and software delivery proceed together; expert help may support early cases but does not replace the owned product.

## Build sequence

**Outcome:** one approved strategy and one selected full-funnel design direction.

Required work:

### Phase 2: owned functional alpha and operator system

**Outcome:** prove the decision method with Matt and no more than five selected users inside the first owned functional slice, with an operator layer for ambiguity and trust.

Build only the smallest real slice that supports:

- structured intake,
- source capture and integrity worksheet,
- Career Baseline,
- employer interpretation,
- evidence and proof review,
- Integrity, Move Value, and Pursuit Readiness,
- Decision Receipt,
- operator time and cost ledger,
- correction and incident log.

Use human review where the system abstains or a consequential ambiguity remains. Record that intervention so the product team can distinguish true self-serve success from operator rescue.

Do not build broad discovery, multi-user collaboration, complex tracking, billing infrastructure, or autonomous application.

Gate:

- at least four of five users accept or materially correct the decision,
- at least three change, confirm, or avoid a real action,
- no critical fabricated fact,
- every unknown remains visible.

### Phase 3: paid product and Guided beta

**Outcome:** collect cash and separately prove owned-software demand, product economics, and optional expert-delivery economics in the narrow beachhead.

Required work:

- My Way Ahead Pro Monthly and Pro 3-Month,
- Expert Decision Review and Guided Career Move,
- transparent scope and payment,
- native Decision Receipt and grounded materials,
- exact approval boundary,
- measured operator time, support, correction, cost, and contribution,
- referral request after value.

Gate:

- at least five of 25 qualified activated users buy the fully disclosed monthly or three-month product plan,
- at least three of 12 qualified prospects buy the $199 Expert Review or $699 Guided beta,
- subscription variable cost remains no more than 20% of subscription revenue and median support remains below 15 minutes per paying user per month,
- expert delivery achieves at least 40% contribution at its standard tested price before scale,
- fewer than 10% refunds,
- no trust or privacy stop event.

### Owned-build boundary after ten paid cases

Before Phase 4, use observed delivery data to decide what to deepen next:

1. which customer-facing workflow belongs in the proprietary core next,
2. which commodity layers should be integrated under portable contracts,
3. where expert review materially improves outcomes and should remain a separately priced option,
4. which features should remain manual, simulated, or deferred because quality or economics are not ready.

Evaluate time to market, user-data control, portability, task quality, support burden, contribution, provider dependence, and rollback. Licensing or white-labeling a competitor's career platform is not one of the paths.

### Phase 4: self-serve core

**Outcome:** users reach a trustworthy first decision without operator assembly.

Build when repeated alpha work justifies it:

- accounts and consent,
- import and progressive verification,
- versioned profile and strategy,
- user-supplied job capture,
- deterministic parsing and validators,
- provider-routed extraction,
- corrections and invalidation,
- Decision Receipt,
- Pursuit workspace,
- billing and entitlement,
- export and deletion.

Gate:

- self-serve users can complete the first decision with acceptable correction and support burden,
- critical red-team suite passes,
- 200-JD alpha benchmark meets field-specific thresholds,
- privacy and security review completed,
- support and source costs fit the offer.

### Phase 5: monitoring experiment

**Outcome:** test whether Opportunity Radar deserves recurring billing.

Build:

- one Radar per user,
- narrow permitted sources,
- direct-source and freshness receipts,
- Brief accounting,
- material-change and no-action states,
- career memory and baseline refresh,
- pause, credits, and reactivation.

Gate:

- among at least ten full-price users, monthly subscribers show credible month-two renewal and matured three-month subscribers show continued use plus informed renewal intent or behavior,
- at least six report material value across two or more Briefs,
- irrelevant surfaced roles remain below 10%,
- no-action value is confirmed by the user,
- contribution supports the channel.

If the gate fails, retain Radar as an episodic product or expert-supported capability rather than the subscription center.

### Phase 6: scale and expansion

Only after the direct method works:

- 1,000-plus JD beta benchmark,
- additional functional lanes,
- interview and offer modules,
- expert marketplace or partner workspace,
- browser-assisted application completion,
- permitted source integrations,
- B2B2C coach or outplacement distribution,
- outcome learning with consent and bias controls.

## 90-day operating roadmap

### Days 0 to 15: instrument Matt and prove the problem

- Freeze Matt's current Career Baseline before recommendations.
- Use TextNow, Going, Tebra, and one materially different role as negative, positive, near-miss, and generalization cases.
- Conduct ten recent-behavior interviews in the narrowed beachhead.
- Run no more than five free owned-product alphas, with operator intervention recorded separately.
- Build the operator intake, Decision Receipt, and measurement ledger.
- Complete the full-funnel visual selection and private prototype without paid inference.

### Days 16 to 30: collect cash

- Offer My Way Ahead Pro Monthly and Pro 3-Month after the free first decision, plus the separately disclosed Expert Review and founding Guided beta.
- Seek three collected payments.
- Record operator time, corrections, decision changes, support, and refund risk.
- Build only minimum profile, job capture, integrity, proof mapping, decision, and export functions.

### Days 31 to 60: prove delivery economics

- Reach ten paying customers across software and expert offers in the narrow beachhead, reporting each revenue type separately.
- Test $49 monthly versus $119 for three months without changing entitlements; test the $199 Expert Review and $699 Guided beta as separate offers.
- Automate only work repeated successfully at least five times.
- Include simulated or manually supported monitoring inside the product experience and record every operator intervention.
- Seek three independent referral sources.

### Days 61 to 90: separate software and retention tests

- Expand self-serve only if trustworthy first decisions no longer require hidden operator assembly.
- Start a separate ten-person full-price monitoring cohort.
- Measure actual second-month renewal.
- Target 10 to 15 paying customers and $5,000 to $10,000 collected.
- Decide the next owned software slice and the right optional expert-support mix based on contribution, quality, and repeatability.

## Technical architecture principles

The prototype host is not the production architecture. Sites is a private review target, not a platform commitment.

### Minimum production layers

| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Responsive web application | Public funnel, account, profile, decisions, pursuits, settings, accessibility, PWA behavior |
| Identity and consent | Authentication, session, consent versions, account recovery, data rights |
| Versioned relational store | Profiles, sources, facts, jobs, decisions, approvals, outcomes, policies, costs |
| Encrypted object store | User uploads and permitted source snapshots with lifecycle deletion |
| Background work queue | Source checks, extraction, adjudication, Radar, document generation, notifications |
| Source connector layer | User-supplied URLs, permitted feeds, browser capture, direct employer verification |
| Model router | Data-class admission, benchmark qualification, budget, capacity, retries, provider audit |
| Deterministic policy engine | Gates, calculations, state transitions, invalidations, approval rules |
| Search and retrieval | Approved profile facts, source spans, taxonomy, prior decisions, career memory |
| Event and learning ledger | Funnel events, corrections, costs, incidents, experiments, outcomes, audit |
| Operator console | Adjudication, customer support, incident containment, source health, beta delivery |

### Architecture choices to defer

- exact cloud and database vendor,
- paid model provider commitment,
- automated source acquisition method,
- browser extension,
- native mobile application,
- multi-tenant coach workspace,
- enterprise identity,
- marketplace or employer integrations.

Select vendors only after the beta reveals source volume, privacy class, concurrency, latency, cost, and operational burden. Favor portable schemas and provider-neutral interfaces.

### Safe observed founder-dogfood claims

- detected an invalid or filled role,
- surfaced a decision-changing gap,
- changed or confirmed a pursuit decision,
- reduced measured decision effort,
- prevented an unsupported claim,

### Unsafe until proven

- improved interview probability,
- accelerated hiring,
- increased compensation,
- produced an offer,
- generalized to other functions or populations.

## Delivery governance

Every initiative records:

- customer and business outcome,
- controlling evidence,
- persona and journey stage,
- hypothesis,
- opportunity value,
- readiness,
- owner,
- current status,
- acceptance criteria,
- dependencies and blockers,
- cost and authority boundary,
- stop condition,
- rollback,
- exact next decision.

Use one writer for overlapping product files. Preserve unrelated user changes. Do not publish, spend, apply, message, or change shared systems without the applicable authority.
