## Audience decision

The launch market is not everyone who wants a job. Start with one economic beachhead, then let onboarding adapt to the person's search mode.

### Initial paid beachhead

U.S. growth, revenue, GTM operations, lifecycle, ecommerce, and adjacent marketing leaders who:

- have roughly 8 to 20 years of experience,
- earn or target about $120,000 to $250,000,
- are employed, recently displaced, or considering a specific opportunity,
- have one live role or a shortlist of two to four,
- face meaningful ambiguity about mandate, proof, compensation, readiness, or direction,
- reject mass apply and value time, credibility, economics, and control.

This is a learning advantage, not the permanent market boundary. Matt's domain knowledge and first case make this group the fastest place to create credible judgment and paid evidence.

## Four decision modes

The modes describe the decision the person is making. They are not fixed personality labels.

| Mode | Current state | Core decision | Failed alternatives | First offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Active Optimizer** | Searching now with a reasonably clear lane | Which real role deserves focused effort, and how do I compete honestly? | Boards, Teal, ChatGPT, resume tools, ATS scans, and spreadsheets used separately | Free first decision, then My Way Ahead Pro or Expert Review |
| **Career Reframer** | Searching or urgent with several adjacent paths | Which version of my background is credible, valuable, and worth the switch? | Assessments, broad searches, generic coaching, and several disconnected resumes | Guided role comparison, then Guided Career Move when useful |
| **Focused Climber** | Employed with a known target level or role | Does my evidence support the next level, and what would invalidate it? | Salary sites, networking, scanners, and generalized coaching | Targeted Decision Review and later Monitoring |
| **Ambitious Pathfinder** | Employed and quietly open across more than one lane | Is anything materially better than where I am now? | Alerts, recruiters, newsletters, and occasional browsing | Monitoring experiment only after active-search value is proven |

### Behavioral overlays

These alter experience design, not acquisition segments:

- **Burnt-Out:** needs low setup, visible progress, save and resume, and no duplicate work.
- **Sophisticated Skeptic:** needs source lineage, uncertainty, correction, version history, and reversibility.
- **Privacy-Sensitive Professional:** needs quiet monitoring, no public signal, narrow permissions, and explicit data control.
- **Signal Builder:** has limited proof and may need career-capital work before opportunity pursuit.
- **Urgent Stabilizer:** needs income quickly and may accept a bridge role that does not maximize long-term upside.

The product must not silently apply a premium-career optimization standard to an urgent survival decision. The user chooses the relevant baseline and time horizon.

## Persona route blueprint

The same platform should adapt the first useful route to the user's decision and evidence maturity. It should not force every person through one long questionnaire.

| Mode | Evidence maturity | Best entry | First useful result | Paid path to test | Return loop | Stop or reroute signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active Optimizer | Usually has a resume and one live role | Paste the role before creating an account | Integrity Preview plus one complete self-serve decision after the minimum Baseline | My Way Ahead Pro, with Expert Review or Guided help when useful | New role, proof gap, application outcome, or recruiter response | Role is inactive, below the economic floor, duplicate, or already pursued |
| Career Reframer | Has substantial experience but fragmented proof across several possible lanes | Import experience, name the trigger, then compare two or three lanes | Supported, transferable, and unsupported evidence by lane | My Way Ahead Pro, with Guided Career Move after a lane is chosen when useful | New evidence, market feedback, or a lane decision | No lane clears credibility or economics; route to career-capital work instead of application volume |
| Focused Climber | Has a current baseline and known next-level target | Define the target role and what materially better means | Readiness boundary, proof gaps, and disconfirming evidence | First self-serve Decision Review when a real role appears; Pro subscription or Expert Review if continuing | Achievement capture, compensation movement, or target-role change | Target would not improve the baseline or requires a hard credential the person will not pursue |
| Ambitious Pathfinder | Has useful history but no urgent role | Define a quiet baseline and a very narrow watch thesis | Career Strategy Brief and a simulated no-action or material-change Brief | Invite to a separately labeled full-price Monitoring experiment only after the active-search method clears its gate; continuation depends on observed renewal | Material market change, readiness change, or a truly exceptional role | Repeated briefs do not save effort or improve confidence; pause without manufacturing activity |
| Urgent Stabilizer overlay | Evidence may be incomplete and the economic runway is short | Declare the survival horizon and non-negotiable floor first | Bridge-role criteria separated from long-term optimization | Scoped review or human triage; do not upsell a premium optimization journey by default | Income stabilization, runway change, or improved option set | The standard path delays viable income or applies long-horizon tradeoffs to an immediate survival need |

Acquisition, onboarding, and success measurement should be segmented by route. A strong result for an Active Optimizer is a trusted decision on a live role; a strong result for an Ambitious Pathfinder may be a credible no-action Brief and an easy pause.

## Jobs to be done

### Active search

> When I need a better role, help me choose and pursue the strongest credible opportunities, so my limited effort goes toward jobs I can win and should want.

### Passive monitoring

> When I am focused on my current work and life, tell me only when a real opportunity could materially improve my options, so I do not miss upside or run a constant search.

### Level-up

> When I am considering a more senior or better-paid role, show whether my evidence supports it and what would invalidate the move, so I act with confidence rather than hope.

### Career change

> When several paths seem possible, compare what the market can credibly believe, what each path costs, and what each path could unlock, so I choose a direction rather than endlessly explore.

## Primary customer journey

| Stage | User question | Product job | Trust proof | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Am I underpaid, exposed, stalled, or missing something better? | Name the costly decision without manufacturing fear | Real examples of stale, weak, and high-upside roles | The user recognizes a current problem |
| Consider | Why not free tools? | Demonstrate one different decision, not another feature list | Integrity and Decision Receipt example | The user understands the upstream decision layer |
| First value | Will this understand me? | Create one useful direction or opportunity insight from minimum data | Sources, uncertainty, and corrections | The user says the result is useful enough to save |
| Baseline | What counts as better? | Define economics, work, constraints, risk, and tradeoffs | User-approved facts and unknowns | A comparison standard exists |
| Strategy | Which paths should govern the search? | Synthesize supported Role Lanes, market thesis, proof, and gaps | Transparent transferability and exclusions | The user approves or corrects the strategy |
| Monitor | Is anything worth attention? | Scan narrow sources and surface only material items | Review accounting, freshness, suppression, and no-action state | The user trusts attention allocation |
| Decide | Why pursue, watch, or pass? | Separate Integrity, Move Value, Readiness, risk, and effort | Source-linked evidence and sensitivity | A real action changes or is confirmed |
| Prepare | What is the strongest honest case? | Build grounded assets and preparation | Every external claim maps to approved evidence | Package is approval-ready |
| Approve | What will happen outside the product? | Show exact payload, destination, action, and revocation rule | Narrow approval receipt | User knowingly authorizes the action |
| Learn | What did the market teach us? | Capture outcomes without false causal inference | Observed event separated from interpretation | Strategy and memory improve |
| Advance | What is better now? | Reset the baseline after a move and continue career compounding | New role, economics, proof, and priorities are approved | User returns when value exists |

## Positioning decision

### Internal category

**Career Opportunity Decision System**

### Plain-language category

**A career opportunity platform for deciding, finding, and pursuing better moves, with expert help when it matters.**

### Positioning statement

For experienced growth and revenue leaders considering a consequential move, the owned platform compares real opportunities with the person's current career and economic baseline, explains the decision with honest evidence and uncertainty, and helps build the strongest defensible pursuit. Expert help is available for consequential ambiguity. Live discovery and monitoring remain product commitments, but they are not public launch claims until source coverage and quality pass their gates.

RoleWorth already occupies much of the low-cost, self-serve `decide before tailoring` position. Waypoint sells human-built senior career intelligence and market maps. Worklore, Umon, and CareerLedger occupy career memory, evidence, and trajectory. Those products establish a quality benchmark, not a prohibition. The launch position is a testable wedge: **baseline-relative judgment and unusually usable guided pursuit for high-stakes moves in functions the team understands deeply.**

### Primary promise

> **Know whether a career move is truly better for you, then make your strongest honest case.**

### Short acquisition promise

> **Bring one role. Leave with a better decision.**

### Mechanism

> Define what better means, verify the real opportunity, compare its value and your readiness, then act with evidence and explicit control.

### Trust line

> **Fresh sources. Honest unknowns. No invented claims. You control every external action.**

### Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** make a better career move.
2. **Pain contrast:** stop wasting effort on noisy, stale, or weak opportunities.
3. **Mechanism:** Career Baseline, Opportunity Integrity, Decision Receipt, expert-guided Pursuit; Radar remains a later validated capability.
4. **Trust:** sources, uncertainty, corrections, privacy, approval.
5. **Proof:** transparent examples and measured founder-case behavior before outcome claims.
6. **Offer:** start with one real opportunity.

Do not lead with `proof graph`, `career OS`, `AI agent`, `model`, `score`, `workflow`, or `always on`. Those are mechanisms or crowded category language.

## Brand strategy

### Desired associations

- forward motion,
- sound judgment,
- selective opportunity,
- economic and career upside,
- calm confidence,
- honest evidence,
- personal control,
- continuity across a career.

### Associations to avoid

- judging the person's worth,
- mass application,
- recruiter or employer surveillance,
- resume keyword gaming,
- robotic automation,
- therapy or personality diagnosis,
- guaranteed outcomes,
- anxiety and constant vigilance,
- finance, healthcare, or legal confusion.

### Voice

- calm, direct, intelligent, and supportive,
- specific without pretending certainty,
- ambitious without hype,
- willing to recommend no action,
- plain language before technical detail,
- respectful of free tools and alternatives,
- never shaming, desperate, or manipulative.

Examples:

- `One opportunity deserves a closer look.`
- `Nothing currently improves on your baseline.`
- `This role has meaningful upside, but one requirement could change the decision.`
- `Your evidence supports the mandate. The job's current status still needs verification.`
- `Pursue starts preparation. Nothing will be sent without your approval.`

## Provisional brand decision

Matt selected **Way Ahead** as the preferred placeholder and identified `mywayahead.com` as the candidate domain. The expert collision screen found that bare **Way Ahead** is too crowded across career guidance, employability programs, an active business-reporting SaaS, navigation software, coaching, and mental health. The governing refinement is therefore:

- **Provisional master brand:** **My Way Ahead**
- **Written form:** always three words; do not shorten it publicly to `Way Ahead`
- **Candidate domain:** `mywayahead.com`
- **App-store form to test:** `My Way Ahead: Career Decisions`
- **Required category descriptor:** `Career decisions for experienced professionals.`
- **Full descriptor:** `A career opportunity platform for deciding, finding, and pursuing better moves.`

The word `My` is strategically important. It signals personal agency, private career memory, and a decision standard owned by the user; it also creates some separation from the crowded two-word phrase and makes the candidate domain natural. This is still a placeholder, not a legal clearance or public-use authorization. If formal review finds that **My Way Ahead** remains too close to existing marks, reject it rather than falling back to bare **Way Ahead**.

On 2026-07-19, Verisign RDAP returned no registration object for `mywayahead.com`, and the domain did not resolve in a DNS check. That supports Matt's point-in-time availability observation but does not reserve the domain, establish registrar availability or price, prove control, or provide trademark clearance. Domain purchase remains an explicit approval action.

### Brand platform

**Brand idea:** Personal progress with proof.

**Core promise:**

> Know whether a career move is truly better for you, then make your strongest honest case.

**Positioning:**

> My Way Ahead compares real opportunities with your current career and economic baseline, explains what holds up and what remains uncertain, and helps you pursue the few moves that deserve your effort.

**Messaging hierarchy:**

1. **Brand line:** `Know which move is truly better.`
2. **Campaign line:** `Your career. Your standard. Your way ahead.`
3. **Conversion line:** `Bring one role. Leave with a better decision.`

Avoid `stay way ahead`, `get ahead of the pack`, `always be ahead`, and similar superiority or guaranteed-outcome language. The brand is about a clearer personal direction, not beating other people.

### Brand behavior

Every surface should express five principles:

1. **Personal, not presumptive:** the user's baseline, constraints, values, and approval govern the decision.
2. **Forward, not frantic:** create momentum without urgency theater, volume pressure, or manufactured activity.
3. **Evidence-led, not clinical:** show sources, unknowns, and corrections in clear human language.
4. **Ambitious, not anxious:** support material career and earning progress without status shame or hype.
5. **Selective, not passive:** recommend pursuit, investigation, watching, or no action when each is the better decision.

### Product architecture

Use the master brand once, then use plain-language modules rather than repeating it in every label:

| System area | Customer-facing modules |
|---|---|
| Strategy | Career Baseline, Role Lanes, Career Strategy Brief |
| Discover | Opportunity Radar, Radar Brief, Opportunities |
| Decide | Opportunity Review, Decision Receipt |
| Pursue | Pursuit Queue, Materials, Application Review |
| Advance | Outcome and Learning, Career Memory |

Plans and services are **My Way Ahead Free**, **My Way Ahead Pro**, **Expert Decision Review**, and **Guided Career Move**. Avoid awkward constructions such as `My Way Ahead Decision Receipt`.

### Visual identity guardrails

The identity should communicate personal clarity, evidence, economic progress, and calm forward motion. Keep **My Way Ahead** visibly separated into three words and pair it with the category descriptor wherever context is weak. Use premium editorial typography, structured evidence, meaningful whitespace, and equivalent light, dark, and system themes. A subtle horizon, baseline comparison, spacing shift, or forward notch may suggest progress. Opportunity Radar may use a restrained field-and-signal metaphor inside the product.

Avoid roads, road signs, map pins, compasses, obvious arrows, races, ladders, graduation caps, AI brains, hearts, and wellness imagery. Those devices are generic and increase confusion with current navigation, education, employment-program, and mental-health uses.

### Identity and typography

- Use the **My Way Ahead** wordmark as the primary launch identity. Do not force an abstract mark to explain an unfamiliar brand.
- For a favicon or app icon, test an **evidence frame** made from two restrained offset rectangular frames divided by one stable baseline. It should communicate comparison and grounded evidence without pointing in a direction.
- Use **Source Serif 4** selectively for marketing headlines and Career Strategy Brief titles.
- Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for the wordmark and product interface, with tabular numerals for compensation and economic comparisons.
- Keep the working interface predominantly sans serif; use no more than two font families.

| Secondary plane | `#F5F8F6` | `#0A0F14` |
| Paper or raised surface | `#FFFFFF` | `#111820` |

Color never carries evidence meaning alone. Every state includes plain-language text and a stable icon or shape.

### Components, motion, and imagery

- Use literal 1.75 to 2 pixel rounded-stroke icons for source, evidence, document, comparison, privacy, alert, filter, and verification.
- Avoid decorative icon circles on every row. Use chevrons only for disclosure or navigation, never as the brand or default call-to-action motif.
- Use 12 to 16 pixel corner radii, one primary action per view, minimum 44 pixel touch targets, and integrated surfaces instead of cards inside cards.
- Use short 120 to 200 millisecond fades, state transitions, and controlled expansion. Avoid scan sweeps, path animations, pulsing glow, directional motion, and celebration.
- Prefer real, quiet decision moments, product evidence, anonymized Decision Receipts, and case-study material. Avoid triumph poses, handshakes, mountaintops, roads, races, wellness imagery, and generic laptop-at-cafe scenes.
- Default appearance to `System`, with labeled `System`, `Light`, and `Dark` settings. An icon-only sun or moon is not the complete control. Theme changes preserve hierarchy, content, dimensions, evidence meaning, and accessibility.

MoveThesis, Career Longview, Opportunity Standard, Worthward, Aheadwise, CareerCompound, and Career Bearings remain evidence in the naming history but no longer govern current design. MoveThesis remains the strongest fallback from the prior screened set if **My Way Ahead** fails validation; it is not automatically approved.

## Naming validation protocol

Use at least 20 target participants across active and passive modes. This is directional screening, not statistical or legal proof. Do not show the logo before the audio test.

1. **Hear-once spelling:** say the complete name once in a product sentence and ask the participant to type it; verify that `My` is retained.
2. **Five-second category:** show wordmark and descriptor, then ask what it does, who it serves, and what result they expect.
3. **Meaning and emotional safety:** ask whether the name suggests personal direction or superiority over other people.
4. **Search retrieval:** after hearing the name, ask the participant to reach the candidate domain without assistance.
5. **Real message test:** use material-opportunity, no-action, correction, and Decision Receipt messages.
6. **Delayed recall:** ask for the complete name and spelling the next day.
7. **Competitive confusion:** ask whether it resembles navigation, therapy, school counseling, recruiting, business coaching, or another known product.
8. **Controlled comparison:** test the same page and visual treatment against the strongest fallback so design preference does not distort the name result.
9. **Formal clearance:** only after user validation, run comprehensive exact, phonetic, common-law, company, state, federal, domain, app-store, social, and counsel review across the relevant software, employment, information, education, and coaching classes.

Suggested advance gate for any finalist, expressed as counts for a 20-person directional screen:

- at least 16 of 20 spell the complete three-word name correctly and retain `My` after hearing it once,
- at least 12 of 20 infer career decisions or materially better opportunities after five seconds,
- no more than four infer navigation, therapy, school counseling, recruiting, business coaching, or another active brand,
- no more than three primarily interpret the name as being superior to other people,
- at least 14 of 20 recall the complete name after 24 hours,
- at least 16 of 20 reach `mywayahead.com` within two attempts after hearing the name,
- the same landing-page design must test all finalists so name effect is not confused with visual treatment,
- formal trademark, common-law, company, domain, app-store, social, and counsel review remains mandatory after user screening.

If users routinely drop `My`, cannot retrieve the domain, misunderstand the category, or counsel identifies material risk, stop public brand lock and advance the fallback instead of using bare **Way Ahead**.

## Offer communication

### Free entry

**Opportunity Integrity Preview plus First Decision**

> Check whether the role is current and complete, then use your minimum Career Baseline to receive one complete self-serve decision with no card.

This proves source quality, interpretation, baseline comparison, and decision clarity. It does not silently include human review.

### Core subscription

**My Way Ahead Pro**

> Keep evaluating, monitoring, and pursuing the few opportunities that can materially improve your career.

Test `$49 monthly` against `$119 every three months`. The three-month plan saves `$28`, or `19.0%`, versus three monthly payments. Show the exact renewal terms and next charge date.

### Expert add-ons

**Expert Decision Review and Guided Career Move**

> Bring a consequential or ambiguous move to a qualified human reviewer, or get guided help turning one decision into an approval-ready pursuit without unsupported claims.

### Continuity experiment

**Opportunity Monitoring**

> Stay focused on your current work. Receive a brief only when the market, your readiness, or a real opportunity materially changes.

Do not sell monitoring as alert volume. It must include fresh source verification, baseline comparison, career memory, decision depth, and credible no-action value. It is part of the product subscription hypothesis, not proof that every user will retain after an active search.

## Objection strategy

| Objection | Product answer, not reassurance copy |
|---|---|
| Why pay when alerts and AI are free? | Show the same opportunity through a generic match and a complete Decision Receipt; charge only if the latter changes a decision |
| Is this another score I cannot trust? | Separate Integrity, Move Value, and Readiness; show sources, unknowns, sensitivity, and correction |
| Will it invent my experience? | Only approved facts can become Proven or externally usable; corrections invalidate dependent work |
| Will my employer know? | Private by default, no public job-search signal, narrow permissions, explicit external approval |
| How much setup will this take? | Progressive import and first value before deep proof; request more only when it can change a live decision |
| Does it find better jobs or only rewrite applications? | Direct-source freshness and material-upside comparison are product gates before preparation |
| What happens after I get hired? | New Career Baseline, achievement memory, compensation movement, readiness, and optional monitoring, subject to retention proof |

## Launch non-targets

- users who primarily want mass apply or autonomous submission,
- resume-template-only and keyword-score-only buyers,
- users unwilling to verify evidence or approve external actions,
- hourly, gig, and shift-market discovery in the first release,
- employers, recruiters, and hiring decision systems,
- legal, immigration, credential, or clinical advice,
- users whose only immediate need is maximum application volume,
- broad audiences where the product lacks domain and taxonomy competence.
