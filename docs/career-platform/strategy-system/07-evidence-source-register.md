# 07 Evidence and Source Register

## Evidence hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Live official sources and directly observed product behavior.
2. Matt's current source-of-truth career files and explicit corrections.
3. Official standards, research papers, regulations, product documentation, pricing, and terms.
4. Structured competitor review patterns with sample and bias limits.
5. Internal strategy synthesis and expert inference.
6. Prototypes and synthetic fixtures, which demonstrate behavior but do not prove demand or efficacy.

Every strategic conclusion should distinguish:

- verified fact,
- vendor claim,
- customer-review signal,
- research finding,
- internal inference,
- hypothesis to validate,
- blocked or missing evidence.

Detailed first-party help flows, public specimens, and official app-store surfaces can demonstrate workflow design. They do not independently establish extraction accuracy, retention, outcomes, or the behavior of a paid authenticated account. Head-to-head tasks remain required.

## Evidence limits

This strategy does not prove:

- product-market fit,
- willingness to pay,
- subscription retention,
- employer-source coverage at scale,
- model accuracy across occupations,
- improved interview, offer, hiring, compensation, or career outcomes,
- legal clearance of any name,
- legal permission for any specific source acquisition method,
- compliance of a future implementation,
- security of a system that has not been built and reviewed.

Those claims require the interviews, paid beta, benchmark, legal review, security review, user testing, and longitudinal evidence defined in the strategy.

## Refresh rules

- Refresh competitor pricing and features before publishing comparisons.
- Refresh any live job source before recommendation, asset preparation, handoff, or application.
- Refresh model pricing, terms, data use, and rate limits before provider selection.
- Review the strategy quarterly or when a major competitor, regulation, source constraint, incident, or beta result changes the recommendation.
- Preserve the old version and issue one clean current strategy instead of accumulating conflicting addenda.
