## Start here

### Executive decision

Build an **owned, mobile-first career opportunity platform** that helps people choose better career moves, find and monitor worthwhile opportunities, and pursue them effectively. Launch with one narrow, high-stakes decision-and-pursuit workflow plus optional expert help. Do not license a competitor's product as the company, and do not begin as another broad job board, resume builder, generic match score, or autonomous application bot.

Concierge delivery is a learning and premium-support layer, not a substitute for building the software. Commodity infrastructure such as hosting, authentication, billing, permitted data sources, and qualified model providers may be bought or integrated when that is safer and more economical. The customer experience, career model, decision policy, evidence system, learning loop, and brand remain owned.

The product should continuously help a person answer four questions:

1. What career moves could materially improve my life and earning trajectory?
2. Which fresh, legitimate opportunities are worth my attention now?
3. Why should I pursue, watch, or pass, and what remains uncertain?
4. If I pursue, what is the strongest honest way to compete and what exactly will happen outside the product?

The user-facing promise is:

> **Know whether a career move is truly better for you, then make your strongest honest case.**

The internal organizing rule is:

> **Improve the decision before accelerating the action.**

Proof, provenance, and human approval remain essential trust infrastructure. They are not the hero message. RoleWorth now publicly combines decision-first scoring, liveness and source checks, proof-backed application packages, approval gates, tracking, Radar, and outcome memory. That makes nearly the entire visible decision-first feature set table stakes, but table stakes still need to be built when they help the user. The product must outperform through correctness, clarity, effort reduction, recovery, decision quality, and repeated real-world usefulness, not through a claim that competitors lack a feature.

### Customer outcome

> **Make better career moves with less wasted effort, less uncertainty, and no need to exaggerate who you are.**

### The shared journey

> **Understand -> Baseline -> Explore -> Monitor -> Decide -> Prepare -> Approve -> Pursue -> Learn -> Advance**

The journey is a loop, not a placement funnel. A new role becomes the next Career Baseline. Outcomes update what the system believes, while user corrections continue to outrank model inference.

### Four product standards

1. **Surface fewer, better opportunities.** Freshness, canonical source, legitimacy, material upside, and user constraints come before volume.
2. **Explain the decision honestly.** Separate facts, inferences, unknowns, transferable evidence, hard gaps, risks, and confidence.
3. **Keep the person in control.** A recommendation, a user decision, product work, and permission for an external action are separate states.
4. **Compound career memory.** Preserve approved achievements, preferences, outcomes, compensation, and lessons so each decision starts stronger than the last.

**Matt, as the Board, owns:** company direction, target customer, brand selection, pricing commitments, spend, hiring, public claims, reserved approvals, and any external job-search action.
**Codex root, as the accountable CEO within delegated authority, owns:** research synthesis, portfolio priorities, dynamic expert routing, product policy, experiment design, artifact quality, build sequencing, QA, economics visibility, risk escalation, and one coherent Board recommendation.

The CEO role is an operating delegation, not a statutory appointment or authority to bind Matt or a future legal entity. Specialists form around bounded missions, read and write versioned shared state, and dissolve when their acceptance and learning are captured. Chapter 11 controls the complete structure.

## Decision register

### Settled for the next design phase

- Build the full funnel, not another isolated job-review screen.
- Treat 34 screen families as the current whole-platform inventory: 30 customer and commercial families plus four operator families; prototype only the 19 starred research families.
- Use one customer system that supports active search, career change, level-up, and passive monitoring through progressive onboarding.
- Keep the initial beachhead to experienced U.S. professionals with meaningful economic upside and decision complexity.
- Separate Integrity Gates, Move Value, and Pursuit Readiness.
- Use a provider-agnostic AI route with deterministic rules, golden-data evaluation, and graceful degradation.
- Preserve explicit light, dark, and system themes with semantic parity.
- Keep Teal out of the customer architecture. It remains competitor intelligence and a temporary first-case-study tool only.
- Build and own the customer product, career data model, decision policy, evidence system, and learning loop; do not license another career platform as the core company.
- Treat feature overlap as a benchmark requirement, not a reason to omit a capability that helps the target user.
- Treat the 19-family full-funnel prototype as concept and usability research, not authorization to build the entire production suite before paid validation.
- Do not publish to Sites until the selected full-funnel prototype passes responsive, interaction, accessibility, and truth QA.
- Preserve the selected Executive Evidence visual identity while rebuilding journey order, spacing, plain language, progressive disclosure, persona-aware inputs, pricing, and fulfillment.
- Preserve the first full-funnel prototype as a superseded comparison baseline. The rebuilt implementation passes local type, build, truth, lint, and regression checks, but its fresh current-build visual gate is blocked until the browser verification runtime is restored.

### Validate before commitment

- Whether Way Ahead passes spoken spelling, category comprehension, emotional safety, confusion, recall, domain-control, collision, and formal legal-clearance tests.
- Whether qualified buyers subscribe to a demonstrably easier and more trustworthy owned product, and which buyers also pay for expert-guided pursuit.
- Whether employed watchers pay for ongoing monitoring outside an active search.
- Whether the Integrity Preview creates enough value to drive sign-up and whether the complete free Decision Receipt creates enough value to drive subscription or expert-offer intent.
- Whether people accept progressive proof verification without feeling interrogated.
- Whether fresh direct-source coverage can remain useful and legally operable at MVP cost.
- Whether the Guided Career Move produces repeatable customer value and reveals product improvements without hiding weak self-serve behavior.
- Whether the provisional Try It Free, Keep Watch, Active Search, Guided Help, and contextual Expert Review architecture is better understood and more economically credible than the previous five-peer offer page.

### Explicitly not approved

- Public launch, paid API commitment, domain purchase, trademark filing, paid acquisition, hiring, mass data collection, autonomous submission, outreach, or any job application.
- Outcome guarantees involving interviews, offers, salary, or time to result.
- An unlimited free AI entitlement or a free-provider route for confidential career data without verified terms and controls.
- Licensing or white-labeling another career platform as the core product.
