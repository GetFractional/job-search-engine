## MentorCruise

### Economics and fit

The [public partner page](https://mentorcruise.com/partners/) states that a partner receives 50 percent of MentorCruise's take rate, described as usually about 10 percent of a qualifying mentee transaction over $50, paid monthly while the referred relationship continues. On the vendor's stated typical $160 to $220 monthly spend, the gross planning proxy is about $16 to $22 per active qualifying referred mentee per month. The vendor also describes an average relationship exceeding three months, which would imply about $48 to $66 or more gross per conversion if attribution, payment, and retention all hold.

Those figures are vendor-derived and incomplete. They are not a forecast, receivable, or approved claim.

Mentoring, practitioner guidance, leadership support, and career coaching fill a current human-service gap without primarily replacing My Way Ahead's career-path discovery, source verification, fit and value decision, evidence continuity, or application-asset workflow. Placement must remain contextual because future staffed coaching could create overlap.

### Customer and privacy evidence

- [Privacy policy](https://www.iubenda.com/app/privacy-policy/77772043/legal), dated 2026-07-14: detailed but broad use of advertising trackers, analytics, session replay, interaction and browsing data, payment data, and listed model processors.
- [Terms](https://www.iubenda.com/terms-and-conditions/77772043), dated 2026-07-14: general customer and platform terms, not a partner agreement.
- [Refund policy](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/46-refund)
- [Pause and cancellation](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/41-pause)
- [Payment effect of ending](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/66-what-happens-do-my-payment-if-i-end-the-mentorship)
- [Flat-fee and inactivity policy](https://help.mentorcruise.com/article/26-flat-fee)

Qualifying service failures may support refunds, but inactivity generally does not. Ending a relationship is immediate and ends access.

### Missing agreement terms

No public partner agreement establishes:

- cookie duration or attribution method;
- payout method, cadence, threshold, currency, or validation delay;
- refund or chargeback commission reversals;
- commission-change notice;
- termination and post-termination commission treatment;
- the contractual meaning of recurring commission language;
- trademark and creative license;
- prohibited traffic and claims;
- geographic eligibility;
- affiliate-platform identity;
- attribution data shared with MentorCruise, its platform, or My Way Ahead.

The partner page also appears stale: it refers to more than 20,000 mentees while a current vendor page refers to more than 50,000. The economic copy must be reconfirmed.

**Verdict:** advance only to an exact Board-approved contact whose sole purpose is obtaining the complete agreement and data-flow documentation. Do not join or publish.

## ResumeSpice

### Economics and mechanics

The current [Awin merchant profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/89733) advertises 10 percent commission and a 15-day cookie. ResumeSpice currently shows base resume packages at [$479, $589, and $699](https://resumespice.com/), implying about $47.90 to $69.90 gross commission on those prices before exclusions.

The separate [program terms](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/89733) conflict: they describe case-specific rates and contain contradictory cookie text that resolves to 60 days. No commission is due on canceled or returned sales, and Awin permits rejection for other invalid transactions. Written confirmation of rate and attribution is required.

Awin generally pays after advertiser approval on the [1st and 15th](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/When-will-I-receive-payment). The [USD threshold](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/What-are-the-payment-thresholds) is at least $20 and defaults to $50. Joining Awin requires a [$5 refundable signup deposit](https://www.awin.com/us/how-to-use-awin/awin-guide-on-how-to-start-affiliate-marketing), which is Board-gated spend.

### Strategic and trust risk

Resume, cover-letter, LinkedIn, interview-prep, and coaching services directly overlap the intended My Way Ahead core. Default promotion could export the highest-value customer work before the company proves its own solution.

The [customer terms](https://resumespice.com/terms-and-conditions/) state that resume-writing sales are final, the advertised guarantee is a rewrite rather than a refund, services expire after 60 days, extensive career and reference information is collected, marketing and behavior tracking services are used, and submitted material receives a broad license unless a separate agreement supersedes it. These are material risks for sensitive career history.

**Verdict:** hold as a narrow, explicit human-resume exception only. Do not join, spend, or make it a default upsell.

## Let's Eat, Grandma

The [affiliate page](https://www.letseatgrandma.com/affiliates/) links to ShareASale merchant ID `145361`. Read-only verification found no current merchant profile; the path falls through to Awin's generic site. No public commission, cookie, attribution, payout, validation, reversal, termination, trademark, geographic, or data-sharing terms were found.

The vendor's resume, cover-letter, LinkedIn, strategy, coaching, and AI-matching services heavily overlap My Way Ahead. Its [privacy page](https://www.letseatgrandma.com/privacy-policy/) is dated 2017, while a different privacy section embedded in the [terms](https://www.letseatgrandma.com/terms/) is dated 2025. The conflict and missing detail about current processors, retention, deletion, file handling, affiliate tracking, and AI use weaken trust.

**Verdict:** do not pursue unless the vendor restores a working program path and supplies coherent current program and privacy terms.

## Required referral controls

Before any eventual referral appears in the product:

- show plain disclosure that My Way Ahead may earn a commission;
- preserve commission-neutral ranking and a non-affiliate alternative;
- use a direct link only, with no profile or behavioral data appended;
- require an explicit user choice before leaving the product;
- summarize the destination's refund and cancellation posture;
- complete an independent service-quality review;
- track gross earned, clawback, net payable, complaint, and cannibalization separately;
- exclude affiliate commission from software MRR and software margin;
- obtain Board approval for the exact program, agreement, disclosure, and placement.
