# Way Ahead Name Knockout Screen

Review completed: 2026-07-21 America/Chicago; source refresh crossed 2026-07-22 UTC
Scope: preliminary collision, domain, category, search, and public-use screen
Legal status: not legal advice and not a comprehensive clearance search

## CEO verdict

**Do not lock or publicly launch Way Ahead as the master brand.** The phrase expresses the intended emotional territory well, but it carries high collision and branded-search risk in software, employability, career planning, publishing, and adjacent services.

Keep `Way Ahead` as the temporary local founder-checkpoint label and preserve the territory through language such as `Your way ahead` while a more distinctive master-name sprint is queued inside Private-Alpha Readiness. This does not create a third active initiative. `My Way Ahead` remains the comparison control, not a cleared fallback.

A missing RDAP object does not reserve a domain, prove registrar availability, establish price, or provide trademark clearance. No domain was purchased or reserved.

### Trademark and market collisions

- USPTO serial [`98396085`](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn98396085) is a live intent-to-use application for the exact standard-character mark `WAY AHEAD`. Its listed Class 41 services are literary publishing rather than this exact software product, but an exact live federal record requires counsel review.
- Exact-name SaaS use exists at [wayahead.be](https://wayahead.be/about).
- Career and employability uses include [Way Ahead career planning](https://www.way-ahead.de/) and [Way Ahead employment support](https://moveon.org.uk/way-ahead/).
- Other material associations include [WayAhead Mental Health Association](https://wayahead.org.au/), [The Way Ahead career publication](https://jpt.spe.org/twa/about-twa), and Gap's [This Way Ahead](https://www.gapinc.com/en-us/articles/2020/07/inspiring-leadership-is-this-way-ahead%21) employment program.
