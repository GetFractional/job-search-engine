# Way Ahead Provisional Name Screen

**Checked:** 2026-07-30 08:55 CDT / 13:55 UTC
**Scope:** preliminary U.S.-focused knockout screen for a multi-user career and
job-search SaaS
**Status:** provisional research only; not legal advice, legal clearance,
domain availability, entity-name availability, registration, or authority to
use the name

## Board recommendation

**Keep `Way Ahead` only as the bounded, non-indexed alpha label and advance it
to formal clearance before any public brand lock.**

This is not a green light. The phrase communicates progress and fits the
customer promise, but it is commercially crowded, weakly distinctive in search,
and already used by active software, career, publishing, media, mental-health,
and mobile-app brands. The primary `.com` is registered. A limited USPTO phrase
query did not surface an active exact `WAY AHEAD` career-SaaS filing, but it did
surface active exact and near-exact marks, including an active registered
`THE WAY AHEAD` mark whose services include recruitment and personnel
placement in aviation. Only a comprehensive clearance search and counsel's
risk opinion can determine whether the name is usable and protectable for the
intended goods and services.

Do not invest in naming-specific SEO, app-store assets, paid acquisition,
standalone-domain purchase, trademark filing, entity-name filing, or a public
announcement yet. Keep product copy and identifiers easy to migrate while a
more distinctive backup name remains in parallel.

### State/entity-name screen limitation

The [Tennessee Secretary of State](https://sos.tn.gov/business-services)
provides a public Business Entity Search by name or control number. Its live
search is session/form-driven, and this bounded no-login screen did not capture
an authoritative exact-name result. Entity-name availability is also separate
from trademark rights. The intended formation state must be checked again
immediately before any entity or assumed-name filing, after the Board selects a
name and approves that filing.

## Risk decision

| Risk | Current level | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal mark conflict | **Unresolved** | Limited phrase results contain active exact and near-exact marks; similarity and coordinated-class review is incomplete. |
| Career/recruitment adjacency | **High enough to require counsel** | Active `THE WAY AHEAD` registration includes recruitment/personnel placement, and active career/media uses exist. |
| Search and app-store confusion | **High** | Exact active mobile app, software product, career service, publication, and nonprofit uses compete for the same phrase. |
| Domain control | **Weak** | Primary `.com` is registered; four longer candidates merely returned no registry object at one moment. |
| Memorability and meaning | **Positive but not unique** | The double meaning is emotionally aligned, easy to say, and easy to spell, but many organizations can and do use it. |
| Migration cost now | **Low** | The product remains alpha-stage and can retain stable internal identifiers while customer-facing naming stays provisional. |

## Exact Board gates

1. **Alpha-use gate:** continue `Way Ahead` only on the bounded,
   non-indexed `wayahead.getfractional.co` alpha. Do not describe it as cleared,
   available, owned, registered, or final.
2. **Clearance-scope gate:** if Matt wants to carry the name toward launch,
   approve the exact U.S. trademark-counsel scope and maximum spend. The scope
   should cover similar sound/appearance/meaning, common-law use, federal and
   relevant state marks, classes 009/035/041/042 and coordinated classes,
   recruitment/career services, SaaS, mobile apps, publishing/education, domain
   and app-store use, and a written risk opinion.
3. **Name-selection gate:** review counsel's opinion together with user
   comprehension, distinctiveness, searchability, app-store confusion, domain
   economics, and at least one more distinctive backup. Matt selects or rejects
   the final master brand.
4. **Control gate:** only after selection, approve the exact domain, registrar,
   term, price, renewal exposure, and registrant; reconfirm RDAP/registrar status
   at purchase time. Entity/DBA, social handles, app-store name, and trademark
   filing each require their own exact action and cost approval.
5. **Launch gate:** do not index, broadly promote, sell under, or announce the
   final brand until domain control, formal clearance, naming migration,
   customer terms/privacy review, and release evidence are complete.
6. **Stop condition:** reject `Way Ahead` before broad launch if counsel finds
   material conflict risk, the usable scope is too narrow, domain/control cost
   is disproportionate, or user testing shows persistent confusion. Preserve
   the product and migrate the label; do not defend sunk naming work.
