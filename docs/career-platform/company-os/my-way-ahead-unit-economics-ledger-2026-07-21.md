## Canonical economics definitions

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Gross billings | Stated price before discount, credit, tax, refund, or chargeback |
| Cash collected | Settled customer cash, excluding uncollected amounts |
| Recognized revenue | Net service revenue earned in the stated period; prepaid terms are recognized across their service period |
| Deferred revenue | Collected cash for service not yet delivered |
| Software MRR | Monthly recurring recognized software revenue only; excludes 30-day nonrenewing offers, one-time cross-sells, affiliate commission, and human service |
| CM1 | Recognized revenue minus refunds and credits, processor cost, and variable product COGS |
| CM2 | CM1 minus support, correction, rework, trust recovery, and attributable human labor |
| Acquisition contribution | CM2 minus channel and customer-acquisition cost |
| Contribution LTV | Realized cumulative CM2 across a mature cohort, not revenue LTV |

Taxes collected for a government are not revenue. Founder labor is an economic cost even when no cash leaves the company. Shared costs require one named, versioned allocation rule and may be allocated once only.

## Free-subsidy control

`maximum free subsidy = conversion rate × conservative realized contribution LTV - other CAC - required margin buffer`

At a 5% conversion rate to one $9 Watch month and 80% CM2, expected contribution is only $0.36 per free entrant. A $0.25 free subsidy leaves $0.11 before any other acquisition cost. At three retained $9 months it is $1.08 before other CAC. Therefore:

- the first free result and Preview draw from the same $0.25 pool;
- eligibility lasts 30 days and requires one verified identity under the future approved abuse policy;
- no repeated free model work is implied;
- when capacity fails, queue, narrow quantity, or return `not processed`; never lower integrity;
- the subsidy is recalculated from mature contribution, not raised from optimism.

## Cohort, refund, CAC, and stop rules

- Track refund rate by customer count and refunded revenue dollars. The private paid test enters review at **10% or more** on either measure and stops immediately for misleading scope, billing, privacy, or quality failure.
- A cost cohort is one offer version, price, term, acquisition source, and entry month. A mature monthly cohort has at least 25 eligible accounts and 30 service days; a prepaid cohort matures after its full service term. Until that sample exists, report observations without statistical claims.
- Stop an offer version after two consecutive mature cohorts exceed its CM2 ceiling. Do not wait two cohorts for a trust, privacy, security, or billing incident.
- Keep Watch conversion is `eligible free entrants who begin a settled paid Watch term within 30 days / eligible free entrants whose free window matured`.
- The 5-of-25 paid-demand gate is a separate qualified-activation experiment and must not be substituted for free-to-paid conversion.
- Paid CAC is $0 today. After mature retention exists, the Board may set a payback window. Any future CAC ceiling uses the lesser of the Board-approved payback contribution and a fixed share of conservative realized contribution LTV.
