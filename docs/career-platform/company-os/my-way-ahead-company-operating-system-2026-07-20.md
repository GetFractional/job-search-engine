## Board resolution

Build and validate an owned, mobile-first career-opportunity company that helps people define a better move, find credible jobs, decide which deserve pursuit, and prepare the strongest honest path forward. The company begins with one narrow, high-value decision instead of attempting every career workflow at once.

Matt is the Board. Codex operates as the accountable CEO and company integrator for research, planning, reversible local execution, expert routing, quality control, and decision synthesis. This is an operating designation, not a legal appointment and not authority to bind Matt or a future legal entity.

The organization will operate like a real company without pretending that virtual roles are employees. Permanent departments own outcomes and policy. Temporary mission pods do bounded work. Roles activate, combine, or dissolve when evidence shows that they improve an outcome enough to justify their cost.

## Executive business plan

### The customer and moment

The commercial beachhead is an experienced U.S. growth, revenue, GTM operations, lifecycle, ecommerce, or adjacent marketing leader who has one live, consequential opportunity and limited time or reputation to waste.

The launch job-to-be-done is:

> When a real role might materially improve my career, but the posting is ambiguous and my time is scarce, help me verify the opportunity, compare it with my current baseline, decide whether it deserves pursuit, and build the strongest honest case.

Hourly workers, early-career users, career changers, level-up professionals, and passive watchers remain legitimate future customers and prototype-comprehension audiences. They are not simultaneous launch markets. The product language stays accessible without expanding the first production ontology beyond what the beachhead can validate.

### The problem

Career decisions are fragmented across job boards, trackers, generic AI, resume tools, salary sites, coaches, and personal spreadsheets. The hardest failures are not a lack of job volume. They are:

- stale, duplicate, wrapper, or noncanonical postings,
- incomplete and contradictory job descriptions,
- required and preferred qualifications being blended,
- compensation components and geography being misread,
- stock equity being confused with fairness or diversity language,
- benefits being treated as guaranteed when eligibility is unclear,
- a generic match score replacing an economic and career decision,
- generated claims outrunning the user's real evidence,
- repeated manual work across every application,
- uncertainty about what AI checked, inferred, omitted, or invented.

### The first sellable outcome

> Bring one real opportunity. Leave knowing whether it deserves pursuit, why, what could break the fit, and the strongest honest path forward.

The product must earn trust through direct-source identity, explicit unknowns, user corrections, evidence lineage, plain-language reasoning, and exact approval before any consequential action.

### The owned advantage

Way Ahead will own the customer product, Career Baseline, career and opportunity data model, decision policy, evidence and correction system, Pursuit workflow, and learning loop. Commodity infrastructure such as authentication, hosting, billing, email, and qualified model capacity may be integrated under portable contracts when authorized. Licensing or white-labeling another career platform as the core company is out.

The company does not assume feature uniqueness. Competitor overlap is a benchmark requirement. The differentiated claim must be proven through execution:

1. compare a job with the person's current reality and definition of better,
2. preserve source truth and consequential uncertainty,
3. separate integrity, move value, and pursuit readiness,
4. turn a decision into grounded preparation without unauthorized action,
5. learn from corrections and outcomes without turning one founder case into general proof.

### Revenue model

Use a staged software-and-partner model:

1. Owned self-serve software remains the company.
2. Keep Watch creates a low-cost recurring entry product only if it provides useful no-opportunity value and observed conversion.
3. Active Search monetizes the high-urgency search and preparation period without forced renewal.
4. Contextual software packs can increase average order value when they finish a distinct customer job.
5. Optional human guidance is referred to a disclosed, qualified partner until the company has real staff and proven fulfillment expertise.
6. Software, affiliate, and any future human-service revenue, cost, demand, and contribution are reported separately.

### Dynamic rewrite rules

- Small, reversible, well-specified task: one qualified owner executes.
- Independent evidence streams: bounded specialists work in parallel; one integrator synthesizes.
- Shared write surface: one writer only; reviewers stay read-only.
- Repeated failure: temporary domain, QA, and risk board repairs only the failed slice.
- Stable low-exception routine: replace fan-out with a script, checklist, test, or single operator.
- High support burden: expose service labor, narrow scope, reprice, or stop.
- Model or source failure: queue, safely reroute, review manually, or return not processed; never imply completeness.
- Material customer or paid-demand shift: re-rank the portfolio and reform mission pods.
- Accepted mission with captured learning: dissolve the pod and retain only the durable owner or automation.

## Automation and quality system

### Automate in this order

1. **Task-packet validator:** reject active tasks missing outcome, DRI, evidence, acceptance, authority, cost, stop, or rollback.
2. **Repo governance checks:** link validity, decision versions, generated-artifact freshness, and authority-conflict detection.
3. **Engineering CI:** typecheck, lint, unit tests, production build, state-machine tests, and exact-approval tests.
4. **Job-intelligence benchmark:** required and preferred; AND and OR; pay components; stock versus fairness language; benefits states; logistics; freshness; canonical employer; duplicates; contradictions; explicit unknowns.
5. **Experience evidence:** required viewport and theme screenshot matrix, matched reference comparison, overflow, focus, keyboard, zoom, reduced motion, and screen-reader semantics.
6. **Cost and model telemetry:** provider, model, policy version, latency, retry, cost, abstention, correction, operator time, and review changes per trusted decision.
7. **Release packet generator:** assemble governing task, diff, tests, fixtures, visual evidence, trust review, cost, and rollback.
8. **Board brief generator:** produce outcome movement, WIP, incidents, economics, recommendation, and exact approvals from canonical sources.

Automation may draft classifications, explanations, tests, task packets, and summaries. It may not autonomously publish, charge, spend, recruit, contact users, change public prices, accept legal risk, process production data, submit applications, send outreach, use references, or negotiate.

### Release gates

1. **Contract gate:** user job, source, states, metrics, accessibility, trust, and acceptance are decision-complete.
2. **Build gate:** typecheck, lint, build, tests, data migrations, and approval state machine pass.
3. **Experience gate:** current-build mobile and desktop, Light, Dark, System, keyboard, zoom, focus, assistive semantics, error, partial, and comprehension checks pass.
4. **Trust and commercial gate:** provenance, unknown preservation, source rights, privacy, billing, cancellation, cost, and consequential-action boundaries pass.

Critical fabrication, false-active status, privacy or security incident, misleading billing, or unauthorized action is a stop event. Schedule pressure cannot waive it.
