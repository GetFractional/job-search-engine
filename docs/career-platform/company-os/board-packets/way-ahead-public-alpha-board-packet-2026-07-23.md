## Customer and product decision

The governing journey is:

> Understand → Set Up → Find → Decide → Prepare → Approve → Apply → Interview → Choose → Learn

The current product closes the P0 safety/coherence slice. It does not yet close
the P1 product promise.

### P1 critical path

1. Structured multi-role source review with accept, edit, reject, provenance,
   conflicts, dates, accountabilities, achievements, projects, skills, and
   tools.
2. A real Career Evidence Library, where résumé bullets are outputs rather than
   the source of truth.
3. Persistent editing for the Job Standard, Job Paths, profile, alerts, privacy,
   and document foundations.
4. System-suggested and user-approved Job Paths, with title aliases and
   exclusions.
5. Stable routes and back behavior.
6. Pursuits through research, package, exact approval, applied, interview,
   offer, outcome, and learning, without employer-facing execution.
7. Clear Master → Job Path → job-specific document precedence.
8. Real How It Works and Tools pages before acquisition scaling.

### P2 validation gates

- Cloudflare Workers AI-hosted Kimi K2.6 is the strongest zero-incremental-
  spend pilot candidate, but no model is connected and free quota is not public
  launch capacity.
- Start with at most two substantive, non-indexed role-guide pilots:
  Revenue Operations Director and Lifecycle Marketing Director.
- No thin programmatic expansion, production email, independent Google or
  LinkedIn OAuth, LinkedIn scraping, paid job data, billing, or public indexing
  is authorized.
