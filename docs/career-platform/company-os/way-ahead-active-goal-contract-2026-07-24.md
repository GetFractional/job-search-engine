## Why this is the right level

OpenAI's current Codex guidance recommends a clear goal, relevant context,
constraints, and a concrete definition of done. It also says goal objectives
must stay below 4,000 characters and that longer instructions should live in a
file referenced by the goal.

This objective therefore names the outcome, controlling sources, acceptance
evidence, and reserved boundaries while leaving the detailed journey, schema,
screen states, critical path, and economics in their governing documents.
