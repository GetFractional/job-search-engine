## Product Case-Study Signals

This Radar already exposes product requirements the platform must preserve:

- official source and canonical employer must be separate from aggregator evidence,
- live status and original posting date can have different confidence levels,
- compensation must distinguish the published band from the user's applicable geo zone,
- a high fit score can remain conditional when one binary application gate is unresolved,
- requirement proof and candidate evidence need separate lineage,
- a higher-paying role should not automatically displace a better-defended decision,
- `Pursue` must not imply permission to create claims, apply, message, or mutate external records.
