## Product findings from founder dogfooding

The live search reinforced six product requirements:

1. Separate role fit, Job Value, Pursuit Readiness, and hiring-outcome
   probability.
2. Make freshness and canonical-employer verification hard gates.
3. Show a scoreboard by Job Path while preserving one integrated next action.
4. Convert missing evidence into small, answerable user gates.
5. Bind analysis and assets to the exact source version and invalidate them when
   the source changes.
6. Keep asset approval, employer-form staging, upload, outreach, references,
   submission, and negotiation as distinct actions.
